toco AI search plan

Fix Toco's site before chasing AI answers

ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI answers decide more of what people buy and where sellers go. Today they can barely read toco.id, and what they can read hurts us. Here is what to fix, in what order, and who owns it.

October 2026 · Based on a toco.id audit on 27 Oct 2026 and ~130 industry sources
1 · The basics

What GEO and AEO actually mean

It's still SEO underneath

AI answers pull from pages that are indexed and readable. If a page can't be crawled, it can't be quoted.

Others talking about us matters most

Mentions of a brand on news sites, YouTube and forums are the strongest signal for being recommended.

Shopping answers run on product feeds

Product picks in AI come mostly from clean product data, such as Google Merchant Center.

Hype to ignore: llms.txt files, "AI schema", rewriting content into small chunks. Studies show none of these move AI citations.

4 · Guardrails

What we won't do

  • Fake posts or buzzer accounts on Reddit, Kaskus or Facebook groups. They get removed fast and damage trust.
  • "Toco is the #1 marketplace" articles on our own site. Google demoted sites doing this in January 2026.
  • Thousands of auto-generated pages like "cheapest X in city Y". Google treats this as spam.
  • Fake ratings or reviews. Ratings must come from real purchases.
  • Sending the full, unfiltered catalog to any feed. One bad seller can get the whole account suspended, as happened on Meta.
  • Saying "gratis" without the Rp2.000 buyer fee. AI repeats half-truths, and the 2026 fee regulation requires full disclosure.

6 · Decisions needed

What we need from Toco to start

  1. The full official fee structureEvery seller and buyer fee, its official name, and the terms of the lifetime fee-free certificate.
  2. A decision on vapesThey are three quarters of today's new listings, and PP 28/2024 restricts selling e-cigarettes online without age verification. Keep with real age checks, hide from search, or remove.
  3. Seller phone numbersStore pages should never carry the number. Confirm buyers only get it through the logged-in, consented contact flow.
  4. Regulation timingWhen the new fee disclosure rule (Permendag 19/2026) takes effect for Toco.
  5. Owners and accessA Tech owner for the site fixes, plus access to Search Console, server logs and Merchant Center.
