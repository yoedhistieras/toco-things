Skincare on Toco is small, but it brings in new buyers

What sold this year, who bought it, and how we can sell more without paying for ads.

1 Jan to 4 Nov 2026Data: Toco orders and Google Analytics

The short version

  1. Skincare is small. 39 orders in October, out of more than 50,000 Toco orders.
  2. It brings new people. 106 of 155 skincare buyers made skincare their very first order on Toco.
  3. Most buy once and leave. Only 25 of 155 bought skincare a second time.
  4. People add to cart, then stop. At the official stores, 428 people added to cart and only 24 orders were paid.
  5. The plan: a skincare page, a quiz, reminders, and "you may also need" offers.

Two personas

A persona is an example customer. We built these two from the numbers above and from what each group buys. They are not real people.

Persona 1

Dimas, 32, Tangerang

The practical man

  • Buys: deodorant and men's face wash (Kahf, Deorex, Odi, Bromen)
  • Spends: under Rp50k per order
  • Shops: weekday afternoon, Android app, pays with QRIS
  • Today: buys one deodorant and does not come back

What brings him back: a face wash offer after the deodorant, and a "time to restock" reminder after about 30 days.

Persona 2

Sari, 29, South Jakarta

The simple-routine woman

  • Buys: face wash, sunscreen, sometimes a vitamin C serum (Emina, Wardah, Dr. Althea, DermAesthetic)
  • Spends: Rp40k for basics, up to Rp190k for a serum
  • Shops: weekday afternoon or evening, app or website, pays with QRIS
  • Today: not sure which products fit her skin, or if a store is genuine

What brings her back: the skincare quiz to build a routine, a sunscreen offer after face wash, and a clear "Official Store" label.

They come, buy one thing, and leave

Skincare is how many people first try Toco. That is good news. The bad news: after the first order, nothing brings them back. Most people buy one product and never buy the next step, like a sunscreen after a face wash.

Few build a routine

Only 24 of 155 buyers bought two or more kinds of skincare, like face wash and sunscreen. They buy cheap, everyday care, not luxury beauty.

Official stores: people visit, few pay

Cetaphil: 60 unpaid orders came from 8 accounts that have never paid for anything on Toco. 2 accounts made 50 of them in a few hours. These are not real shoppers, so we left them out above and sent them to the payment team to check.

Kahf: 291 people put Kahf in their cart. Only 12 paid. They need a reminder.

7What we will do

No paid ads. We use what Toco already owns: the app, the website, messages, and brand partners.

4. "You may also need"

After someone buys, we offer one related product:

  • Deodorant → men's face wash
  • Face wash → sunscreen
  • Sunscreen → face wash
  • Body wash → deodorant
  • Vitamins → sunscreen
