12Quick fixes

Fix the leaks before we bring more people.

1

Cart reminder

Remind people 1 hour and 1 day after they leave a cart. Start with Kahf: 291 carts, 12 paid.

2

"Time to restock"

A deodorant lasts about a month, a face wash about 6 weeks. Remind them before it runs out.

3

Check suspicious accounts

8 accounts made 60 unpaid Cetaphil orders and never paid for anything. The payment team should review them.

4

Brand-paid vouchers

Ask Kahf, Wardah, Emina and Cetaphil to fund a launch voucher. Toco gives the shelf space.

5

Clean the shelf

Move items that are not skincare out of the skincare categories.

6

Show "Official Store"

68% of buyers are new to Toco. They need to trust that the product is real.
