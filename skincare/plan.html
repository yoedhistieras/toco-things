What to expect

Small wins first, bigger wins later.

1. Clean the categorySkincare numbers will look smaller at first. That is normal.Quick
2. Introduce skincareSmall numbers at first. In-app only reaches people who open the app.Quick
3. Partner with brandsDepends on each brand saying yes.Slower
4. Page and quizThe biggest reach, once it is inside the app.Biggest
5. Sell the next productWe will only see results after people run out, about 30 days.Slower
