Problem 1
Critical

Risky listings fill the index

In one line

Every day, tens of thousands of new vape listings, along with abortion-pill spam, prescription drugs and alcohol, go out to Google and AI crawlers as normal pages that are open to indexing.

What we found

  • Today's newest-listings sitemap has 40,000 URLs. 75.0% are vape by the basic keyword match, and 86.4% with the extended classifier. Across the whole site, about 2.50M listings are vape-type, which is 27.6% of 9.07M. Heated tobacco and cigarettes add another 100,890.
  • The uploads come in bursts. 7 of the last 8 days were burst days, with 94K to 203K new listings each and 52% to 62% of them vape. On quiet days vape is about 1%. October alone (to 28 Oct) brought 1.78M new listings, 47.5% of them vape.
  • Today's file comes from 4 stores: loka-gaya, mode-kita, wawa-velvet and nawa-club. All 4 were created within 9 minutes on 2026-10-01, all list Kota Bekasi, and all uploaded profile pictures within one hour on 10-03. All 73 sampled vape listings came from them. No listing was moderated, and the bulk-upload flag was never set.
  • The vape is filed under Elektronik > Elektronik Rumah Tangga > Setrika (clothes irons): 80 of 94 fetched vape pages. 91 of 94 have is_adult=false.
  • 54 abortion-pill listings are live, all returning 200 with index, follow. 51 come from one store, ddfsdyd, created 2026-10-13 and still not banned. Its store description advertises abortion pills in UAE cities. Two new Indonesian misoprostol listings appeared on 10-27 and 10-28.
  • Prescription and controlled drugs are listed: alprazolam 1 mg from a store named alprazolam-1mg-ready (filed as "Vitamin C"), diazepam (some disguised as "case hp kode valisanbe" phone cases), codeine 20 mg, and about 1,100 "jual viagra/cialis asli" doorway pages. Listings set to show=false are still served with index, follow and stay in the sitemap.
  • Alcohol sits in a real category, Produk Dewasa > Minuman Beralkohol, even though Toco's own policy bans "Minuman beralkohol". The age gate is client-side only: the server HTML has no age marker, so crawlers get title, photos and markup in full.

Example duplicate pair, same title, same price, created 6 minutes apart by two network stores: foom-lite-mango (mode-kita) and foom-lite-mango (wawa-velvet).

How to see it yourself

  1. Open either example listing above. Press Ctrl+U (Cmd+Option+U on Mac) to view the page source.
  2. Press Ctrl+F and search robots. You will see index, follow. Search is_adult and you will see false.

To count vape in today's sitemap (one command, any Mac terminal):

curl -s https://sitemaps.toco.id/sitemap-listings-recent.xml.gz | gunzip \
 | grep -oE '<loc>[^<]+' | grep -ciE 'vape|liquid|saltnic|salt-nic|relx|foom|oxva|freebase|vapor|atomizer'

Out of 40,000 URLs, the result today was 30,012.

Why it matters

  • Legal. PP 28/2024 Pasal 434 bans selling tobacco and e-cigarettes through commercial websites and apps (ayat 1 huruf f). Ayat 2 exempts them only "jika terdapat verifikasi umur" (if there is age verification). A guest checkbox that vape pages never even show is a weak basis for that exemption. To be confirmed by Legal. Vape liquid is also an excise good (PMK 193/2021, amended by PMK 96/2024), and listings without excise stamp details add illegal-excise risk. Online alcohol sales under Permendag 20/2014 as amended: to be confirmed by Legal.
  • Our own policy. The product policy bans "Narkotika, psikotropika, obat keras", "Minuman beralkohol", "Rokok dan produk tembakau" and "spam atau duplikasi tidak wajar". The bulk networks break the duplication rule, so Toco can act on them under today's wording.
  • Search and AI. Google Merchant Center's dangerous products policy does not allow tobacco or e-cigarettes. OpenAI's commerce guide lists age-restricted products (alcohol, nicotine) and prescription-only medicines as prohibited. AI crawlers that read our sitemap see a catalogue that is about one quarter nicotine.
  • Trust. Abortion-pill spam and coded psychotropics under the Toco name are a press and regulator risk on their own. They also carry brand "Toco" in their markup (see Problem 4).

Root cause

Uploads are not moderated and stores are not risk-scored, so a few freshly created stores can push 40K listings in two hours. The vape is filed under Setrika with is_adult=false, and the age gate only fires on is_adult or the "Produk Dewasa" category, so it never triggers. The sitemap generator includes every listing, hidden ones too, and the only age control runs in the browser, which is not an SEO control.

How to keep a page out of the index

Taken down: return 410

For removed listings, return HTTP 410 (or 404) and drop the URL from every sitemap the same day. A 410 tells search engines the page is gone for good.

Allowed but restricted: noindex

Send <meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow"> or the X-Robots-Tag: noindex header, and remove the Product markup.

Filter the sitemap

The generator should leave out restricted categories, is_adult listings, blocklist hits, risky stores, hidden listings and duplicates.

Stop it at upload

Run the classifier and a store risk score when listings are created, so the next network never reaches the index.

Urgent cases: Search Console Removals

Use the Removals tool to hide the worst URLs in Google quickly while the 410 takes effect. It is temporary, not a fix.

Not robots.txt Disallow

Disallow stops crawling, so Google can never see the noindex or the 410. Blocked URLs can stay indexed from links, just without a description.

# restricted but allowed (e.g. if vape is kept with real age checks)
X-Robots-Tag: noindex
<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow">

# taken down
HTTP/1.1 410 Gone

Problem 2
Critical

AI can't read our key pages

In one line

The home page, About, FAQ, product policy, category pages and the "0% biaya admin" article are blank to any crawler that does not run JavaScript, and that includes ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity.

What we found

  • 0 visible characters on all 8 pages we tested, read the way GPTBot reads them. Home, About, FAQ, policy, the article and /p/dapur also have no title, description, canonical or structured data.
  • The result is identical for OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Googlebot and desktop Chrome. Nothing blocks bots, and robots.txt allows everyone. The page is simply empty until JavaScript runs.
  • Listing and store pages are partly better: they have head tags, and listings carry Product markup. Their body text is still 0.
  • The FAQ content comes from an API response of 910 KB, and 94% of it is 7 base64-embedded images. The real text is about 50.7K characters across 81 questions.
  • Every page says <html lang="en">, even under /id/, although the content is Indonesian.
  • Missing listings, stores, categories and articles return 200 instead of 404. A missing store even says index, follow and points its canonical at the fake URL.
  • Every page is sent as private, no-store, so CloudFront misses on every crawler hit.

How to see it yourself

curl -s -A "GPTBot/1.2" https://toco.id/about | grep -o '<title>[^<]*'

Why it matters

  • AI visibility. GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot fetch raw HTML and do not run JavaScript, according to the vendors' crawler docs and the Vercel/MERJ crawler study (Dec 2024). Our main pitch, "tanpa biaya admin", is invisible to them on the very pages that explain it, so questions like "apa itu Toco" are answered from third-party sites or competitors.
  • Google and Bing. Googlebot does render JavaScript, but in a second, delayed queue. On a 9M-URL site that costs coverage and freshness. Bing, and with it Copilot, renders less reliably.
  • Wasted work. The SEO work already exists. Organization, WebSite and NewsArticle markup, titles and canonicals all appear after JavaScript, so crawlers that skip JavaScript never get them. Soft 404s also burn crawl budget on a site with millions of listing URLs.

Root cause

The shared MainLayout component returns nothing until a browser-only effect sets mounted=true. It was probably added to hide hydration warnings from a screen-size check. Almost every page wraps both its content and its <Head> SEO tags inside this layout, so the server sends an empty shell. A second layer sits on top: FAQ, policy and article text are fetched in the browser, the listing body is marked client-only, and _document hard-codes lang="en".

// today (simplified): nothing renders on the server
const [mounted, setMounted] = useState(false);
useEffect(() => setMounted(true), []);
return mounted ? <div>{header}{children}{footer}</div> : null;

// fix: always render; gate only browser-only widgets
return <div>{header}{children}{footer}<ClientOnly><FloatingChat/></ClientOnly></div>;

How to fix it

  1. F4. Set the page language. Change _document to <Html lang="id">.
    Do firstTechS
  2. F1. Remove the layout gate. Render MainLayout on the server. Make the screen-size hook SSR-safe (initializeWithValue: false, or CSS breakpoints), wrap only truly browser-only widgets in a small <ClientOnly>, and move every <SEO/>/<Head> outside the layout, as listing and store pages already do. Most of the effort is hunting hydration mismatches and QA across templates, premium storefronts included.
    Do firstTechM
  3. F2. Fetch content on the server. FAQ, product policy (and likely terms and privacy) via getStaticProps with revalidate. The article already has its data in props, so seed the query with it. Move the FAQ's base64 images to CDN URLs first, or the HTML grows by about 1.8 MB.
    TechS each
  4. F5. Return real 404s. In getServerSideProps, use return { notFound: true } for missing listings, categories, articles and stores. Store and article need a server-side existence check.
    TechS per route
  5. F6. Structured data in the server HTML. Organization and WebSite appear automatically after F1. Add FAQPage (81 questions, plain text), BreadcrumbList on category, listing and article pages, and Store on store pages. Do not output the CMS articleMeta.seo string as-is: it has an empty description and raw HTML in the body.
    TechS
  6. F3. Server-render the listing body. Or, as a smaller fallback, server-render a static block with H1, price, condition, description, store and rating.
    LaterTechM to L
  7. Optional F7. Cache content pages at the edge. For About, FAQ, policy and articles, use public, s-maxage=600, stale-while-revalidate=86400 or ISR.
    LaterTechS

Overall: M, about 1 to 2 sprints for one frontend engineer plus QA. F4, F1 and F2 alone make home, About, FAQ, policy and articles fully readable to every AI crawler.

Problem 3
High

Seller phone numbers are exposed on store pages

In one line

For sellers who switched phone display on, the public store page hands their phone number to anyone. That skips the login and consent step Toco built for buyers.

What we found

  • The seller's toggle is respected. When visible_phone_number = false, the number is not in the page data (10 of 10 stores).
  • When the toggle is on, the number is in plain text in the store page data (36 of 36), three copies per page. The store page never shows it: there is no tel: link, no WhatsApp link, and the store code never reads the field. It is sent by accident.
  • Toco's intended flow is gated. On a listing, "Hubungi Penjual" requires login, then acceptance of the contact terms, then a call to an authenticated endpoint, then a modal with a fraud warning. The store page skips all three steps.
  • Harvesting is cheap. The same data comes from /_next/data/{buildId}/store/{slug}.json as about 4 KB of JSON. All about 141,662 store slugs are in public sitemaps, and 90% of sampled stores had a number, so roughly 109K to 136K numbers are reachable by one unauthenticated crawler in a few hours. We saw no rate limiting.
  • Analytics leak. When a buyer clicks a contact option, the seller's number is sent to PostHog (US cloud) as the event property seller_phone_number.
  • Only the phone leaks. Listing pages are clean (0 of 20), and we found no email, address, ID number or bank details in any page data.

How to see it yourself

  1. Use an internal test seller with phone display switched on. Open its store page on toco.id while logged out.
  2. Press Ctrl+U for view-source, then Ctrl+F toco_phone. Today you will find the number three times. After the fix it should find nothing.

Please do not test on real sellers' stores or copy any number you see.

Why it matters

  • Legal: UU PDP. Under UU No. 27/2022, a mobile number tied to a store name and city identifies most individual and UMKM sellers. Toco's own consent flow defines the purpose as contact from a logged-in, consenting buyer, so anonymous bulk exposure goes beyond it (purpose limitation, Pasal 27 to 28; security, Pasal 35 to 39). Full obligations have applied since October 2024. Administrative sanctions reach up to 2% of annual revenue (Pasal 57). Sending the number to PostHog in the US touches the cross-border rules in Pasal 56. The status of the implementing PP and the PDP agency is to be confirmed by Legal.
  • Seller safety. Fake-buyer WhatsApp scams are common in Indonesia: fake payment proofs, and malware APKs disguised as shipping receipts. A list of 100K+ seller numbers tagged with store, city, category and an "online now" signal is an ideal target list. Toco's own modal says it does not cover fraud outside the app.
  • Already copied. Crawlers that store raw HTML (Common Crawl, Internet Archive, SEO and AI-training crawlers) have likely captured this data. Treat old copies as already out there. This is reasoned, not verified.

Why High and not Critical: the seller's own visibility setting is honoured, only the phone number leaks (no ID, bank, address or email), nothing is cached at the edge, and no secrets are exposed. It is still a real control bypass that affects about 90% of listed stores.

Root cause

The store page's getServerSideProps passes the raw store-detail object from the backend straight into page props, three times (profile, storeMetaData and the react-query cache). Next.js then prints those props into every page and its data route. The upstream store API probably returns the phone to anonymous callers too. We did not call it directly, so Tech must check.

How to fix it

  1. Whitelist the public store fields. Map the store through one toPublicStore(), use it for all three copies (or drop the duplicates), and leave out toco_phone. This also fixes the data route. 
    const toPublicStore = s => ({ id: s.id, toco_name: s.toco_name,
  toco_short_name: s.toco_short_name, toco_city: s.toco_city, /* other display fields */
  visible_phone_number: !!s.visible_phone_number }); // bool only, never the number
    Do firstTech0.5 to 1 day
  2. Remove the phone from the PostHog event (and ideally the seller name), keep seller_id, and delete the property's history in PostHog.
    Do firstTechUnder 1 hour
  3. Fix it at the source. The public store-detail API should not return the phone to anonymous or unprivileged callers. The number should only come from /api/store/phone/{store_id}.
    Tech0.5 to 1 day
  4. Harden the phone endpoint on the server. Require login and contact consent, return 404 when the toggle is off, rate-limit per user and per IP, and log reveals for abuse detection. Today the gate is enforced in browser code.
    Tech0.5 to 1 day
  5. Add a regression test and a nightly check. Fail CI if store props contain phone, email, ID or bank keys or values. Each night, sample 50 sitemap stores and 50 listings on production with the same check.
    TechAbout 0.5 day
  6. Audit similar routes. Search and category pages, premium store pages (check that contact_info.phone is a business contact published on purpose), classified ad detail, buyer profiles and review authors, and order or share-card endpoints.
    TechS
  7. Settle consent. Confirm the toggle wording and its default. If the default was on, ask sellers again. If a public contact channel is wanted, use in-app chat or a masked relay number.
    LaterProductLegal

Total: about 2 to 4 engineering days, low risk, because the store page never reads the field and the listing flow already fetches the number from its own endpoint.

Done when

  • For a test store with the toggle on, both the store page HTML and its /_next/data JSON contain 0 toco_phone keys and 0 phone-pattern matches.
  • The same holds for 50 random sitemap stores and 50 random listings.
  • "Hubungi Penjual" still works for a logged-in buyer who accepted the terms.
  • An anonymous request to /api/store/phone/{id} returns 401 or 403.
  • PostHog events no longer contain seller_phone_number.
  • The check command and CI test spec are written and can be handed to Tech.

Decisions needed

  • Consent default. 90% of sitemap stores have the toggle on, but only 40% in the listing sample. That gap suggests a default or legacy value rather than an active choice, which weakens consent under Pasal 22. Confirm the default and the toggle text. Owners: Product, Legal.
  • Notification. Whether this counts as a protection failure that triggers the written notice duty in Pasal 46 (3 × 24 hours). Owner: Legal.
Problem 4
High

Every product is labelled brand "Toco"

In one line

Every product page tells Google, Meta and AI shopping systems that the product's brand is "Toco", whether it is Jameson whisky, a Dreame vacuum or a vape liquid.

What we found

  • 205 of 205 fetched listings emit "brand":{"@type":"Brand","name":"Toco"}. That includes vape, alcohol, Rx drugs and the abortion-pill spam.
  • The listing data has no brand field at all (no brand, merk or attribute fields, and variants empty on 205 of 205). The value is hard-coded.
  • 28 of 50 non-vape titles do name a real brand, but guessing it from the text fails. A simple dictionary match was wrong in at least 5 of 9 hits: "compatible with iPhone" or "Cover Bolt CVT Vespa" would be labelled Apple or Vespa.
  • The markup is thin: always the same 7 keys, never seller, sku, gtin, ratings or breadcrumbs. Only the first photo is included, and the description is a 160-character cut that ends mid-sentence ("Cocok untuk ").
  • 0 of 205 listings have any reviews, so no rating markup is allowed today.
  • Across 9.07M pages, "Toco" is declared the brand of about 2.4M vape products, plus alcohol, Rx and misoprostol.

Example used below: the MAKADAM camping spice set, sold by the official MAKADAM store, and still labelled brand "Toco".

How to see it yourself

  1. Open any listing, press Ctrl+U, then Ctrl+F "Brand". You will see "name":"Toco".
  2. Or paste the listing URL into Google's Rich Results Test and open the Product item. Brand reads "Toco".

Why it matters

  • Google policy. Structured data "must be a true representation of the page content", and violations can lead to a manual action (general structured data guidelines). Merchant Center allows the store name as brand only if you manufacture the product, and says to leave brand out when in doubt (brand attribute). A wrong brand reads as misrepresentation, and a third-party brand on a knock-off reads as counterfeit.
  • We already paid for this. Earlier in 2026, Toco's Meta catalog took 3 trademark and counterfeit strikes after pixel-ingested listings carried brand "Toco". The Meta pixel reads the same on-page markup, so fixing the markup also fixes that path.
  • AI shopping. The OpenAI product feed spec requires brand "as shown on the product page" and a seller name, with the seller's own page for marketplaces. Today we give the wrong brand and no seller.
  • Entity confusion. AI systems learn brand-to-product links from this markup, so "Toco" becomes a co-brand of vape liquids and misoprostol. That is a GEO problem and a risk to the Toco mark itself.

Root cause

The Product markup builder hard-codes brand: "Toco" because listings have no brand field to read from. The same builder only outputs the first image and the short description, and never adds the seller.

How to fix it

  1. Omit brand on all third-party listings now. Keep "Toco" only for products Toco itself makes or private-labels.
    Do firstTechS
  2. Make the markup complete. Add offers.seller (store name and store URL), sku (seller SKU, or the listing ID as fallback) and productID, all photos as an image array, the full description trimmed at a word boundary, availability from stock, and condition. Add BreadcrumbList, using only crumbs whose category URL returns 200. Output no Product markup at all on restricted or noindexed listings.
    TechS to M
  3. Ratings only when real. Add aggregateRating or review only when at least one real review is visible on the page.
    TechS
  4. Add a seller-declared brand field. At upload, a picker from a brand registry plus "Tanpa merek / generic". Output brand only when it is declared, is not "Toco", and is not contradicted by "compatible with / untuk / for" in the title. Optionally require authorisation for the top protected brands, which ties in with policy section D, "Pelanggaran merek". Never derive brand from free text.
    LaterProductTechTrust & SafetyLegalM to L
  5. Re-check the Meta catalog after the markup ships, and confirm no new brand-related strikes.
    LaterMarketingS

In one line

Toco has no one page that states its fees, its own pages contradict each other, and when people ask for a "marketplace tanpa biaya admin", AI answers conclude that none exists.

What we found

  • Fee wording appears in 107 of 511 articles. None of it is in server HTML. The /faq has 75 questions and no seller-fee question. The seller landing page's fee answers exist only in a JavaScript bundle.

  • In 9 answer-engine searches, no toco.id URL appeared, not even for site:toco.id biaya admin. For "marketplace tanpa biaya admin 2026", Toco is absent. One summary said it found no official Toco fee schedule.
  • Shopee's fee page, "Ringkasan Jenis Biaya" (dated 31-07-2026), has a fee-by-seller-type table with totals and footnotes, and ranks #1 for Shopee fee queries.
  • From 25 Oct 2026, Permen UMKM 3/2026 makes other platforms cut admin and commission fees by at least 50% for verified UMK. Toco's seller-side Rp0 is still lower, and our page should say so honestly.
  • toco.id/biaya returns 404 today, so the URL is free.

  • AI visibility. The generic query is won by national business news (Katadata, Bisnis, Detik, Kontan) and by official fee pages such as Shopee's. No outlet links to an official Toco fee page because none exists, and 2026 coverage has started to drop the Rp2.000 detail.

  • Trust. "Tanpa potongan apapun" next to real deductions, and "seumur hidup" next to a terms clause that allows change at any time, are contradictions a journalist, regulator or AI answer can quote back at us.

Root cause

The fee model changed over two years (Rp0 for everyone, then a parking fee announced, then web only, then all transactions), and each change was described in new articles without updating the old ones. No page is the official source, no one owns the wording, the pages that do exist are client-rendered, and /terms was never reconciled with the lifetime certificate.
